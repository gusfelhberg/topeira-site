Iara: River of Lanterns

Guide a lantern up five real rivers of the Amazon and bring it home to Iara, the keeper of the waters in Brazilian folklore.

One tap pushes the lantern. Frogs, caimans, jaguars and the pink river dolphin are each on the river where they really live, and every river tells you something true about it. In Zen the river is dark, nothing strikes, and your light wakes what lives there.
