Support: Iara: River of Lanterns

Email hello@studiotopeira.com and, if you can, tell us which phone you have and its system version. We read every message.

Does the game need an internet connection?

Not to play: it works fully offline. A connection is needed only to buy more rivers or to restore a purchase.

Is the game free?

It is free to start: the first 25 rivers are free. There are no ads, no subscription and no account.

What can I buy?

More rivers: Into the night (rivers 26 to 60), six expansions of 25 rivers each, or all of them at once. Each is bought once, inside the game, and is yours to keep. You can try the first river of each before buying.

How do I get my purchases back on a new phone?

Open the game's Settings and choose "Restore purchases". A purchase belongs to the Apple or Google account it was made with, and does not carry between iPhone and Android.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google, whichever store the purchase was made in. Ask through that store.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. Everything it remembers is stored on your device, so removing the app removes all of it. Purchases stay with your Apple or Google account and can be restored.

A river feels impossible

Zen mode has the same rivers with nothing that strikes. If a spot in Play really cannot be passed, please write and tell us which river: that is a bug, and we will fix it.

How do I change the language?

In the game's Settings (the gear on the first screen): English, Português or Español.
