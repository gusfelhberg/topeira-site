Support: Iara: River of Lanterns

Email hello@studiotopeira.com and, if you can, tell us which iPhone and iOS version you have. We read every message.

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. It works fully offline and version 1.0 makes no network calls at all.

Is there an account, a subscription or anything to buy?

No. The game is free, with no ads and no purchases.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. Everything it remembers is stored on your device, so removing the app removes all of it.

A river feels impossible

Zen mode has the same rivers with nothing that strikes. If a spot in Play really cannot be passed, please write and tell us which river: that is a bug, and we will fix it.

How do I change the language?

In the game's Settings (the gear on the first screen): English or Português.
